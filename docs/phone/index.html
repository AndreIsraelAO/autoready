Get notifications on your phone

AutoReady will ping your phone when it accepts a match, so you're back in time.

    It delivers AutoReady's notifications. No account needed.

    It's your private channel. Don't share it or show it on stream.

  3. Open ntfy and tap +

    It's at the top right of the main screen.
