Get notifications on your phone

AutoReady will ping your phone when it accepts a match, so you're back in time.

  1. Get the free ntfy app

    It delivers AutoReady's notifications. No account needed.

    Get it on the App Store

  2. Copy your code

    It's your private channel. Don't share it or show it on stream.

  3. Open ntfy and tap Allow

    It asks the first time you open it. Without this, nothing arrives.

  4. Tap + at the top right

    On the Subscribed topics screen.

  5. Paste the code and tap Subscribe

    Touch and hold the Topic name box, tap Paste, then Subscribe at the top right.

  6. Send a test from your PC

    In AutoReady, click Send test. When it arrives here, you're done.

Still stuck? See the step-by-step help

  1. Connect ntfy

    Opens the free ntfy app and subscribes you. No app yet? Google Play opens first: install it, then come back and tap again.

    Connect with ntfy

  2. Allow notifications

    If ntfy asks, tap Allow. That's it.

  3. Send a test from your PC

    In AutoReady, click Send test. When it arrives here, you're done.

Alerts arrive late? Check battery settings

Some Android phones pause apps in the background to save battery. In Settings → Apps → ntfy → Battery, choose Unrestricted (the name varies by brand) so alerts arrive right away.

Didn't work? Do it by hand

Copy the code, open ntfy, tap +, paste it in Topic name and tap Subscribe.

This link is missing your code

Open AutoReady on your PC, turn on Phone notifications, click Show code and scan the QR with your phone camera.
